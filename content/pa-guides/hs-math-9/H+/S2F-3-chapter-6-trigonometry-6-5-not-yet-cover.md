“Radian measure”: number of radius units in the length of corresponding arc.

Let A be degree measure, B be radian measure, then:

  - The initial ray is always located on the positive $x$-axis.
- Angle direction:
  - Counterclockwise rotation → positive angle
  - Clockwise rotation → negative angle
- The amount of rotation determines the measure of the angle.

## 6.2 Trigonometric ratio of arbitrary angles

As in a unit circle r=1, we have cos(a) = x, sin(a) = y, and tan (a) = y/x.

“Reference Angle”:

- The reference angle is the **acute** angle formed between the terminal side and the x-axis
- Acute angle a is the reference angle for 180-a, 180+a, 360-a as well as all coterminal angles.
