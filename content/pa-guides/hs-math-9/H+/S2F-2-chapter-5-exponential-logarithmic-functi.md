## 5.3 Exponential function

Form: f(x) = bx

Separate into 2 cases:

- 0<b<1

Strictly decreases, has H.A. of y=0, y intercept at 1.

- 1<b<pos infinite

Strictly increases, has H.A. of y=0, y intercept at 1.

Possible questions: Find range of functions: transfer the function into standard form, analyze the domain and its corresponding range.
