**Basic Terms:**

-Circle, center, radius, diameter,…

-Minor arc: arc smaller than semicircle.

-Major arc: arc wider than semicircle.

-Semicircle: half a circle.

-Tangent: line that intersects circle at one and only one point.

-Concentric: adj, (circles) sharing a same center

**Postulates:**

-Arc addition postulate: measure arcAB+arcBC=measure arcAC.

-Area of a circle=r^2pi, circumference=2rpi.

**Theorems of Circle:**

-Two segments tangent to a circle, joining at one point, are equal in lengths.

-(a) A diameter perpendicular to a chord bisects the chord and its arcs (b) A diameter that bisects the chord is perpendicular to the chord (c) The perpendicular bisector of a chord contains the diameter of the circle.

-The measure of an inscribed angle=half the measure of its intercepted arc.

-Corollary: 2 inscribed angles that intercept the same arc are congruent.

-Corollary: An angle inscribed in a semicircle is 90 degrees.

-Corollary: The opposite angles of a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle are supplementary.

-The measure of an angle formed by a tangent and a chord=half the measure of the intercepted arc.
