# S1MPA-3 3.1 Properties of Parallel Lines

> From the Grade 9 Resource Department's Semester 1 Midterm (PA) Maths S+ review guide (PA). Writers: Mickey 9(5). Editor: Michelle 9(11). Range: 1.0-4.6. Student-made reference material: where it differs from class, follow class.

1.Corresponding Angles Postulate

If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, then corresponding angles are congruent.

2.Alternate Interior Angles Theorem

If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, then alternate interior angles are congruent.

3.Same-Side Interior Angles Theorem

If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, then same-side interior angles are supplementary.

4.Alternate Exterior Angles Theorem

If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, then alternate exterior angles are congruent.

5.Same-Side Exterior Angles Theorem

If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, then same-side exterior angles are supplementary.

## 3.2 Proving Lines Parallel

1.Converse of the Corresponding Angles Postulate

If two lines and a transversal form corresponding angles that are congruent, then the two lines are parallel.

2.Converse of the Alternate Interior Angles Theorem

If two lines and a transversal form alternate interior angles that are congruent, then the two lines are parallel.

3.Converse of the Same-Side Interior Angles Theorem

If two lines and a transversal form same-side interior angles that are supplementary, then the two lines are parallel.

4.Converse of the Alternate Exterior Angles Theorem

If two lines and a transversal form alternate exterior angles that are congruent, then the two lines are parallel.

5.Converse of the Same-Side Exterior Angles Theorem

If two lines and a transversal form same-side exterior angles that are supplementary, then the two lines are parallel.

## 3.3 Parallel and Perpendicular Lines

1.If 2 lines are parallel to the same line, then they are parallel to each other.

2.In a plane, if two lines are perpendicular to the same line, then they are parallel to each other.

## 3.4 Parallel Lines and the Triangle Angle-Sum Theorem

1.Triangle Angle-Sum Theorem

The sum of the measures of the angles of a triangle is 180.

2.Triangle Exterior Angle Theorem

The measure of each exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the measures of its two remote interior angles.
