## 8.5

**Angle of elevation**: The angle formed between a horizontal line from an observer and the line of sight to an object above the horizontal line.

**Angle of depression**: The angle formed between a horizontal line from an observer and the line of sight to an object below the horizontal line.

[The angle of elevation and angle of depression to the same object are congruent (equal), as they are alternate interior angles formed by parallel horizontal lines.]

**Other essential details of** **Chapter 8:**

Objective: To use trignometry to find unknown sides or angles of a right triangle.

In a right triangle, when certain parts of a triangle are known, trigonometric relationships can be used to find the unknown parts. The lengths of corresponding sides are proportional, and we have the equations:

$$\sin \theta =\frac{\text{ opposite }}{h\text{ ypotenuse }}$$

$$\cos \theta =\frac{\text{ adjacent }}{h\text{ ypotenuse }}$$

$$\tan \theta =\frac{\text{ opposite }}{\text{ adjacent }}$$
