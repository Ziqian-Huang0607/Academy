If two angles of a triangle are congruent to two angles of another triangle, then these two triangles are similar.

## 4.2 Triangle Congruence by SSS and SAS

1.SSS

If all three sides of a triangle are congruent to all three sides of another triangle, then these two triangles are congruent.

2.SAS

If two sides and an included angle are congruent to two sides and an included angle of another triangle, then these two triangles are congruent.

## 4.3 Triangle Congruence by ASA and AAS

1.ASA

If two angles and an included side of a triangle are congruent to two angles and an included side of another triangle, then these two triangles are congruent.

2.AAS

If two angles and a side of a triangle are congruent to two angles and a side of another triangle, then these two triangles are congruent.

## 4.4 Using Congruent Triangles: CPCTC

If two triangles are proven congruent by three congruences, then the other three congruences hold too because all parts of congruent triangles are congruent.

## 4.5 Isosceles and Equilateral Triangles

1.Isosceles Triangle Theorem

If two sides of a triangle are congruent, then the angles opposite of those sides are congruent.

2.Converse of Isosceles Triangle Theorem

If two angles of a triangle are congruent, then the sides opposite of those sides are congruent.

3.The bisector of the vertex angle of an isosceles triangle is the bisector of the base.

4.Corollary to Isosceles Triangle Theorem

If a triangle is equilateral, then the triangle is equiangular.

5.Corollary to Converse of Isosceles Triangle Theorem

If a triangle is equiangular, then it is equilateral.

## 4.6 Congruence in Right Triangles

HL Theorem

If the hypotenuse and a leg of a right triangle are congruent to the hypotenuse and a leg of another triangle, then these two triangles are congruent.
