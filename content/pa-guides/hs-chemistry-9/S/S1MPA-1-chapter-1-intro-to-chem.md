Introduction

- “Chemistry”: comes from the word “alchemy”

-Chemistry is the study of:

1. the composition of matter

2. the changes (chemical/physical) that matter undergoes

-Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space

-Composition is the chemical identity of a substance

Branches of Chemistry:

Organic Chemistry--contains carbon

Inorganic Chemistry --no carbon

Biochemistry--biological

Analytical Chemistry--composition

Physical Chemistry--energy TRANSFER
