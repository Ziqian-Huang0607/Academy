**1. Rules for Rounding Numbers (Only for lab results)**

-If the number right next to the last sig. fig. is less than 5, don’ t change the last significant figure.

-If the number right next to the last sig. fig. is greater than 5, round up the last significant figure

-If the number right next to the last sig. fig. is equal to 5 and is followed by a non-zero, round up the last significant figure

-If the number right next to the last sig. fig. is equal to 5 and is not followed by a zero/nothing, look at the last significant figure

**2. Accuracy and precision**

-Accuracy: how close to truth value (Accepted value)

-Precision: the repeatability of the measurement using a given instrument (集中)

**3. Error**

-Error = experimental value – accepted value

-

**1) Systematic error**

- one direction low accuracy, can’t be reduce by repeating

-mis-calibrated device/wrong measuring technique

-e.g.: Reaction time, zero error

**2) Random errors**

-both direction, low precision, can be reduce by repeating

- **Least count:** smallest division that is marked

- **ILE** (Instrument limit of error): digital=least count, Analogue= ½ of least count

**(Measured value ± uncertainty) unit**

-Uncertainty: 1 sf

-Measured value’s last sf is in the same place as the uncertainty.

-Measured value and uncertainty have same power of ten

**Absolute uncertainty:** △x, size of error and units

**Precent uncertainty:** △x/x ×100% (keep have 1-2sf)

**Error:** Measured value-expected value

**Precent error:** |error|/ expected value (may have 1-2sf)

**Conversion Factors**

-a ratio of equivalent measurements

-have an unlimited number of significant figures and do not affect the rounding of a calculated answer
