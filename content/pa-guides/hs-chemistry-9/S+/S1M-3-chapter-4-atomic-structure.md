vii. Rutherford's Gold Foil Experiment:

According to the experiment, A narrow beam of alpha particles was directed at a very thin sheet of gold

a. In his original hypothesis, based on Thomson's model, alpha particles should pass through gold foil in nearly no deflection.

b. However, the actual results displayed that a small fraction of the alpha particles bounced off the gold foil at very large angles. Some even bounced straight back toward the source. c. This incredible result is impossible to be interpreted by Thomson's model, so his model fails.

The Rutherford's Gold Foil Experiment revealed atoms consist mostly of empty space with a dense, positively charged nucleus at their center.

3 . Distinguishment Among Atoms

Atomic Number: the number of protons in the nucleus, that identifies an element.

Isotopes: atoms that have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons are called isotopes

e.g. Hydrogen atoms have three isotopes

protium (0 neutron)

deuterium (1 neutron)

tritium (2 neutrons)

Isotopes of the same element have essentially identical chemical properties

The major difference between isotopes is their mass
