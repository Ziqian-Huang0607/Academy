**7.2 Effective Nuclear Charge + 7.3 Sizes of Atoms and Ions**

-attraction (nucleus) and repulsion (other electrons), nuclear charge depends on both

- **Shielding effect**: The repelling effect that inner electrons have on outer (valence) electrons, resulting a decrease in the amount of attraction felt by the valence electrons

-Effective nuclear charge refers to the charge felt by the outermost (valence) electrons after considering the shielding effect

-Zeff outer>zeff inner due to the screening of inner electrons

-s>p>d>f in the same principal energy level.

**Trends in the Periodic Table**

-in a period: more protons=increasing effective nuclear charge=stronger attraction force=more condensed, smaller atoms

-in a group: more principal energy levels=more layers=thicker=increased radii

**Size of Ions**

-Cations: valence electron removed=repulsions between electrons are reduced=atoms become smaller

-Anions: valence electron added=more repulsions=atom size increases

-isoelectronic series: same number of electrons, ionic size decreases with an increasing

nuclear charge
