- Literary tradition: a system of written communication.

- Language family: Largest group. Languages share an ancient common ancestor, from way before recorded history.

- Language branch: Within one family. Shared ancestor thousands of years ago; differences are smaller than between separate families.

- Language group: Within one branch. Shared origin in the recent past; similar grammar & vocabulary.

- Estimates 6,909 languages worldwide.

- Only 11 languages (English, Mandarin, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Lahnda, German) are widely known.\

Language families

Indo-European

The most widely used language family

Main distribution: Europe, South Asia, North America, Latin America

Sino-Tibetan

Main area: China + several Southeast Asian countries

Mandarin—most used

Other large Sinitic languages (≥20 million speakers): Wu, Min, Yue (Cantonese), Jinyu, Xiang, Hakka, Gan → Mostly in southern & eastern China

- Chinese uses logograms: symbols that represent words/ideas, not sounds

ASIAN LANG FAMILIES

Austronesian

Distribution: Indonesia, Madagascar, Pacific islands

Javanese—most widely used in Indonesia

- Many languages here have long history of seafaring migration.

Austro-Asiatic

Main language: Vietnamese

- Uses the Roman alphabet modified with diacritics (accents/tone marks) → adapted from Catholic missionaries.

- Main location: Vietnam, Cambodia.

Tai-Kadai

- Mainly in Southeast Asia (Thailand, Laos).

- Thai is the major language.

- Uses logograms historically (similar idea to Chinese writing), now its script is different.

Japanese

Korean

Languages of SW Asia and N africa and central asia

Uralic

European non-Indo-European family.

- Languages: Finnish, Hungarian, Estonian.

- Origin: Central Asia; migrated to Europe long ago.

- These are surrounded by Indo-European languages but kept their own language identity.

Altaic

- Main: Turkish, Mongolian.

- Turkish switched from Arabic script to Latin alphabet

- Once spread across Central Asia.

African Language Families

1. Afro-Asiatic

Southwest Asia + North Africa

- Biggest language: Arabic

- Arabic is the main language of Islam; one of the UN official languages.

- Hebrew also belongs to Afro-Asiatic.

2. Niger-Congo

Largest African language family by number of languages

- Covers most of sub-Saharan Africa.

- Famous language: Swahili

- Swahili is a lingua franca in East Africa.

- Mix of African languages + Arabic loanwords.

3. Nilo-Saharan

- Small number of speakers, scattered across north-central Africa.

- Languages are not widely used.

4. Khoisan

- Famous for click consonants — unique clicking sounds.

- Very few speakers today; endangered
