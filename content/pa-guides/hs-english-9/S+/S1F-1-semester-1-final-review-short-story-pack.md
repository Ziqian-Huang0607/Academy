- **Concepts:**

**Perspective:**

—> In literature, a perspective is a lens through which leaders observe characters, events, and happenings.

-- First-person perspective -> from narrator’s perspective

-- Second-person perspective -> make audience a part of the story, let them feel personally involved and morally responsible

-- Third-person perspective -> to describe what’s happening with the characters and what they’re doing

**Tone (authorial voice):**

—> A term that describes how the writer expresses their attitude or feelings about the subject, characters, or events of a literary work through their word choices.

**Mood:**

—> refers to the emotional response that the writer wishes to evoke in the reader through a story

**Theme & Thematic statement:**

Theme -- one word or phrase that concludes what the story is generally talking about

(e.g. Family, Culture, Identity, Food, International racism)

Thematic statement -- a full sentence or statement which gives a detailed but concise explanation of what the story is trying to say

(e.g. Ordinary comfort is tied to other people’s suffering.)

**Character Arc**

—> how a character changes or develops throughout a story (or not)

--> can be negative, positive, or static

—> Show through actions and / or relationships

**Brief Summary**:
Mr. Maloney told Ms. Maloney that they are divorcing. Then Ms. Maloney killed her husband using a lamb leg, and she was not suspected. The lamb was eaten by the detectors while finding the murderer.

**POV**:

- Third-person perspective (Ms.Maloney)

**Perspective (age, race, gender)**:
2. Females who are weaker in the marriage

**Theme + Statement**:
It is acceptable to resist your husband if he divorces with you.

**Symbols (+ significance)**:
Lamb: sacrifice tool, fake or hypocritical symbol
Significance: to reflect Ms.Maloney's actions of acting innocent in the story.

**Inner Conflict**:

- Conflict between Ms.Maloney and her husband in marriage & love

**External Conflict**:
2. Ms.Maloney tried to hide the truth with the policemen of her crime.

**Tone/Mood Changes + Why**:
Tone: joyful -> abnormal -> suspicious
Mood: happy, bright -> awkward -> strange -> anxious -> suspicious

**POV**:

- First-person (Amy)

**Perspective (age, race, gender)**:
2. Fourteen, Chinese, female

**Theme + Statement**:
Be yourself. Do not feel shame for your ethnicity. Do not exchange your uniqueness blindly for someone else' heart.

**Symbols (+ significance)**:
Fish cheek: the softest part of the fish which eat commonly in China.
Significance: old-fashioned Chinese tradition & love from Amy's father

**Conflict**:
Conflict between original culture and new culture.
Should Amy accept their own culture in the face of possible discrimination?

**Tone/Mood Changes + Why**:
Tone: apprehensive -> embarrassed -> despairing -> upset -> grateful
Mood: anxious -> unhappy -> uncomfortable -> awkward -> delighted
To highlight the growth of Amy
