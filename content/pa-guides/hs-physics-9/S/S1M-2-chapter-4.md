**1.** A car travels 20 km north and then 35 km south. What is the car’s total displacement?
a) 15 km north

**2.** On a position-time graph, what does a straight line with a positive slope represent?
a) An object at rest
b) An object moving with constant positive acceleration
c) An object moving with constant positive velocity
d) An object moving with constant negative velocity

**3.** Which of the following is a correct statement of Newton's First Law?
a) For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.
b) The acceleration of an object is inversely proportional to its mass.
c) An object in motion will eventually come to a stop.

**4.** A ball is thrown straight up into the air. At the very top of its trajectory, what is true about its velocity and acceleration? (Ignore air resistance.)
a) Velocity is zero and acceleration is zero.
b) Velocity is zero and acceleration is 9.8 m/s² downward.
c) Velocity is 9.8 m/s upward and acceleration is zero.
d) Velocity is 9.8 m/s downward and acceleration is 9.8 m/s² downward.

**5.** A student draws a free-body diagram for a book resting on a level table. Which forces should be included?

**6.** A driver traveling at 25 m/s slams on the brakes, coming to a stop in 5.0 seconds. What was the car's average acceleration during this braking period? (Steps Required)

**10.** A 4.0 kg box is being pulled across a frictionless horizontal surface by a horizontal force of 12 N. What is the acceleration of the box? (Steps Required)

**Answer Key:**

- **b) 15 km south**
  - Solution: Define north as positive. Displacement = +20 km + (-35 km) = -15 km. A negative displacement south is 15 km south.
- **c) An object moving with constant positive velocity**
  - Solution: On a position-time graph, slope = velocity. A straight, positive slope means a constant, positive velocity.
- **d) An object will maintain its state of motion unless acted upon by a net force.**
  - Solution: This is the definition of Newton's First Law (the Law of Inertia).
- **b) Velocity is zero and acceleration is 9.8 m/s² downward.**
  - Solution: At the top, the ball has momentarily stopped, so velocity is zero. However, gravity is still acting on it, so its acceleration is always 9.8 m/s² downward.
- **b) The weight of the book and the normal force from the table.**
  - Solution: A free-body diagram only includes forces acting on the system (the book). The weight (Fg, down) and the normal force (FN, up) are the two forces acting on the book. The "force of the book on the table" is a force acting on the table, not the book.

**Calculation Problem Solutions:**

**6. Acceleration during Braking**
Solution:
 vi = 25 m/s, vf = 0 m/s, Δt = 5.0 s
Use a = (vf - vi) / Δt
a = (0 - 25) / 5.0 = -5.0 m/s²
**Answer: The car's acceleration was -5.0 m/s².**

**7. Projectile Motion**
Solution (Assume UP is positive, g = -10 m/s²):*
vi = 12 m/s, vf = 0 m/s (at max height), a = -10 m/s²
**a) Time to max height:**
Use vf = vi + a*t
0 = 12 + (-10)*t
10t = 12
t = 1.2 s
**b) Maximum height:**
Use Δd = vi*t + (1/2)*a*t²
Δd = (12)(1.2) + (1/2)(-10)(1.2)²
Δd = 14.4 - 7.2 = 7.2 m
**Answer: a) t = 1.2 s, b) max height = 7.2 m**

**8. Forces on a Skydiver**
Solution (Assume DOWN is positive):
m = 70 kg, g = 9.8 m/s², Fair = -500 N (upwards, so negative)
**a) Weight (Fg):** Fg = m*g = 70 kg * 9.8 m/s² = 686 N (downward)
**b) Net Force (F_net):**
Fnet = Fg + Fair = 686 N + (-500 N) = 186 N (downward)
**c) Acceleration (a):**
Use Fnet = m*a
186 N = 70 kg * a
a = 186 / 70 ≈ 2.66 m/s² (downward)
**Answer: a) Fg = 686 N, b) Fnet = 186 N down, c) a ≈ 2.7 m/s² down**

**9. Displacement from v-t Graph**
Solution:

Displacement = Area under v-t graph.
From t=0 to t=4s: Area = 5 m/s * 4 s = **+20 m**
From t=4 to t=8s: Area = (-5 m/s) * 4 s = **-20 m**
Total Displacement = +20 m + (-20 m) = **0 m**
**Answer: The total displacement is 0 meters.**

**10. Newton's Second Law**
Solution:
m = 4.0 kg, Fnet = 12 N (frictionless surface)
Use Fnet = m*a
12 N = 4.0 kg * a
a = 12 / 4.0 = 3.0 m/s²
**Answer: The box's acceleration is 3.0 m/s².**
