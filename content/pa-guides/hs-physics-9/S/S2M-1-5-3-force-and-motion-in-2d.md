**Equilibrant:** a single force that, when added to a system, makes Fnet = 0

- cancels out rest of the vectors, so should always be

equal in magnitude, opposite in direction with the vector(s)

**Vector Resolution Steps:**

- free body diagram:
- define a define a coordinate system
    - x axis along the slope, y axis perpendicular to

When the object is along a slope (the hw questions) there is: Fn, Fg, and depending on the question, Ff (friction)

- Fn is perpendicular to the surface of the slope
- Fg points directly down, perpendicular to the ground
- Ff only when the object is moving (kinetic friction), or was supposed to move but held in place (static friction), along the surface of the slope, opposing motion

(“sliding down”, “moving”, (gives a kinetic friction), “slows down”)

- Resolve Fg

Since Fg is the only force that is not strictly along the x or y axis, break it into x and y components so it goes along the x or y

  - solve for Fx and Fy using sine and cosine
    - Fg is the hypotenuse of the triangle

the Fx leg is horizontal parallel to the ramp

Fy leg is vertical perpendicular to the ramp

    - sine()= opposite leg (to theta) / Fg (hypotenuse)

opposite leg (to theta) = sin() * Fg (hypotenuse)

    - cosine()= adjacent (to theta) / Fg (hypotenuse)

adjacent leg (to theta) = cos() * Fg (hypotenuse)

- Find magnitude of Fnet = √Fx2 + Fy2
- Find angle =tan-1 |Fy / Fx | above the x axis (or whatever you chose to name ur system)
