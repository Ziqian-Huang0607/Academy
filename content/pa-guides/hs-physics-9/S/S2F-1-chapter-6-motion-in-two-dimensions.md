## 6.1 Projectile Motion

- A **projectile** is an object shot through the air while ignoring air resistance
- The curved path that a projectile follows is called its **trajectory**
- The most important idea is the **independence** **of** **perpendicular** **components** — the horizontal motion doesn’t affect vertical motion, and the vertical motion also doesn’t affect horizontal motion.
- When launched horizontally, the initial vertical velocity is zero
- Horizontally: no net force → accelerationx = 0 → **constant** **horizontal** **velocity**
- Vertically: net force is weight → ay = g → **free** **fall**
- The same time t will be used for both directions
- Horizontal distance(range): dx = vixt
- Vertical distance (height): dy = (1/2)(gt2)
- Vertical velocity: vy = gt
- Final velocity magnitude: v = (vx2 + vy2)1/2
- Final velocity direction: θ = tan-1(vy / vx) below horizontal

**Note**: Use g = 9.8 Downward is defined as the positive direction.

Example Questions:

1. A tennis ball rolls off the edge of a 1.30 m high table and lands 0.42 m from the base.

- To find the time used to fall: use dy = (1/2)(gt2)
- 1.30 = (1/2)(9.8)t2 → t = 0.52 seconds
- To find horizontal velocity: use dx = vixt
- 0.42 = vix(0.52) → vix = 0.81 m/s
- To find final vertical velocity: use vy = gt
- vy = (9.8)(0.52) = 5.10 m/s downward
- To find final velocity magnitude: v = (0.812 + 5.102)1/2 = 5.16 m/s
- To find final velocity direction: θ = tan-1(5.10 / 0.81) = 81.98° below horizontal

2. A stone is thrown horizontally from the top of a 25 m cliff. The stone lands 80 m from the base. Find the initial horizontal velocity.

- First find time from height: 25 = (1/2)(9.8)t2 → t = 2.3 seconds
- Then use range: 80 = vx (2.3) → vx = 35 m/s

3. Stones A and B are thrown from a 100 m cliff horizontally. Stone A at 4 m/s, Stone B at 8 m/s. Find their velocities 1 second later.

- For stone A: vx = 4 m/s, vy = (9.8)(1) = 9.8 m/s
- Magnitude: vA = (42 + 9.82)1/2= 10.6 m/s
- Direction: θ = tan-1(9.8/4) = 67.8° below horizontal
- For stone B: vx = 8 m/s, vy = 9.8 m/s
- Magnitude: vB = (82 + 9.82)1/2 = 12.6 m/s
- Direction: θ = tan-1(9.8/8) = 50.8° below horizontal

## 6.2 Circular Motion

- **Uniform** **circular** **motion** means moving around a fixed radius circle at constant speed
- Centripetal force is not an independent force. It is supplied by tension, friction or gravity.
- Even with constant speed, the object accelerates because direction changes
- **Centripetal** **acceleration** always points toward the center
- Magnitude: ac = v2 / r
- Also: ac = 4π2r / T2 where T is the period
- Period and frequency: T = 1 / f
- Speed from period: v = 2πr/T
- **Centripetal** **force** is the net force toward the center: Fc = mac = mv2 / r = 4π2mr / T2
- Fc is not a new kind of force — it is the name for the real force that points toward the center
- Friction provides Fc for a car on a flat curve
- Tension provides Fc for a ball on a string
- Gravity provides Fc for the moon around Earth
- Centrifugal force is the apparent outward force of a rotating object
- Centrifugal force doesn’t exist — the outward feeling is just inertia
- Maximum speed on a flat curve without slipping: vmax = (μsrg)1/2
- For vertical circular motion at the top: Fc = Fg + FN = mv2 / r
- Minimum speed at the top to avoid falling: vmin = (gr)1/2
- If v < (gr)1/2 at the top, the object will drop from its circular path

Example Questions:

1. A 13 g rubber stopper is attached to a 0.93 m string and swung in a horizontal circle, making one revolution in 1.18 seconds. Find the tension force.

- Use Fc = m(4π2 r / T2)
- Fc = (0.013) (4(3.14)2(0.93) / (1.18)2)
- Fc = 0.34 N toward the center

2. A 150 g ball revolves uniformly in a horizontal circle of radius 0.6 m, making 2.00 revolutions per second. Find centripetal acceleration.

- First find period: T = 1/f = 1/2 = 0.5 seconds
- Use ac = 4π2r / T2 = 4π2(0.6) / (0.5)2
- ac = 94.7 m/s² toward the center

3. A car racing on a flat track travels at 22 m/s around a curve with a 56 m radius. Find centripetal acceleration and the minimum coefficient of static friction needed.

- Centripetal acceleration: ac = v2 / r = (22)2 / 56 = 8.6 m/s²
- To find μs: friction provides centripetal force → Ff = mac
- Also Ff ≤ μsFN and FN = mg
- So m(8.6) ≤ μsmg → μs ≥ 8.6 / 9.8 = 0.88

4. A carnival clown rides around a vertical loop of radius 18 m. What is the slowest speed at the top to avoid falling?*

- Use vmin = (gr)1/2 = ((9.8)(18))1/2
- vmin = 13.3 m/s

## 6.3 Relative Velocity

- **Relative** **velocity** relates measurements from different observers (different frames of referene
- Use subscript notation: vA/B means velocity of A relative to B
- Key equation: vA/C = vA/B + vB/C
- The middle letter cancels out, similar to fraction multiplication
- To solve: label all objects, identify the relative velocity phrases and write the velocities with proper subscripts. When objects move along the same straight line, add velocities for same directions, subtract for opposite directions.

Example Questions:

1. You are in a bus traveling at 8 m/s in a positive direction. You walk toward the front at 3 m/s relative to the bus. How fast does a friend on the ground say you are moving?

- vyou/ground = vyou/bus + vbus/ground
- vyou/ground = 3 + 8 = 11 m/s

2. A person walks along a moving sidewalk at 3 m/s in the same direction the sidewalk moves. The sidewalk moves at 2 m/s relative to the ground. Find the person's velocity relative to the ground.

- vperson/ground = vperson/sidewalk + vsidewalk/ground
- vperson/ground = 3 + 2 = 5 m/s

3. The same person turns around and walks opposite direction at 3 m/s on the same moving sidewalk. Find velocity relative to ground.

- vperson/ground = -3 + 2 = -1 m/s (moving backward relative to ground)

4. An airplane flies due north at 150 km/h relative to the air. There is a wind blowing at 10 m/s to the east relative to the ground. Find the plane's speed relative to the ground.

- First convert 150 km/h to m/s: 150(1000/3600) = 41.7 m/s north
- Use Pythagorean theorem because north and east are perpendicular
- vground = ((41.7)2 + (10)2)1/2 = (1738.89 + 100)1/2 = (1838.89)1/2 = 42.9 m/s
