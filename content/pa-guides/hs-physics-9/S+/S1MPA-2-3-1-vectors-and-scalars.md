### Pythagorean Theorem

When $\theta = 90^\circ$ between vectors A and B:

$$R^2 = A^2 + B^2 \qquad \beta = \arctan\frac{B}{A}$$

where $\beta$ is the angle between R and A.

### Law of cosines and law of sines

When $\theta \ne 90^\circ$:

- **Law of cosines:** $R^2 = A^2 + B^2 - 2AB\cos\theta$
- **Law of sines:** $\frac{R}{\sin\theta} = \frac{A}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{B}{\sin\beta}$

## 3-3 Subtraction of Vectors, and Multiplication of a Vector by a Scalar

**Negative vectors:** two vectors are negatives of each other if they have the same magnitude but point 180° apart (in opposite directions): $\vec A = -\vec B$.

Adding the negative of vector B to vector A is subtracting B from A:

$$\vec A - \vec B = \vec A + (-\vec B)$$

For example, with final position $d_f$ and initial position $d_i$, the displacement is $d_f - d_i = \Delta d$. Method 1 reverses $\vec B$ and adds it tip to tail; method 2 places $\vec A$ and $\vec B$ tail to tail and draws $\Delta d$ from the tip of $\vec B$ to the tip of $\vec A$. (See the 3.3 PPT for more examples.)

**Multiplying a vector:** a vector $\vec A$ can be multiplied by a scalar $c$.

- If $c$ is **positive**, $c\vec A$ has the same direction and magnitude $cA$.
- If $c$ is **negative**, $c\vec A$ has magnitude $|c|A$ and the opposite direction.

## 3-4 Adding Vectors by Components

For a right triangle with hypotenuse C, side A opposite $\theta$ and side B adjacent to $\theta$:

$$\sin\theta = \frac{A}{C} \qquad \cos\theta = \frac{B}{C} \qquad \tan\theta = \frac{A}{B}$$
