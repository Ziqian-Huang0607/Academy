**5-1 Kinematics of Uniform Circular Motion**

**Uniform Circular Motion:**

- Definition: An object that moves in a circle at constant **speed** (**velocity** not constant)
- Velocity vectors are always **tangent** to the circular path

**Centripetal Acceleration**

- Always points towards the center of the circle
- aR = v2r-1 or ω2r or 4π2rf2

**5-2 Dynamics of Uniform Circular Motion**

**Centripetal Force**

- Definition: The **net** force toward the center of the circle when an object moves in a circle
- FR = maR
- **Not** a new kind of force; net force of circular motion
  - R ∝ r
  - FR ∝ ω2
  - FR ∝ m

**Centripetal Force (DOES NOT EXIST!!!)**

Definition: Inertia caused by Centripetal Force

**Loop Formulas** **on A CIRCULAR PATH:**

vmax = (gr)0.5

**5-3 Highway Curves, Banked and Unbanked**

**Unbanked Curves**

- When a car goes around on an unbanked curve, Fnet = Fsf = FR = mv2r-1
- If static friction isn’t enough, the car will tend to move in a straight line, or **skid.**

**Banked Curves**

- Formulas:
  - V = (gr(tanθ))0.5
  - FNm-1 = g(cos(θ) - μssin(θ))-1 [MAX SPEED]
  - FNm-1 = g(cos(θ) + μssin(θ))-1 [MIN SPEED]

**5-6 Newton’s Law of Universal Gravitation**

**Statement:**

Every particle in the universe attracts every other particle with a force that is proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them. This force acts along the line joining the two particles.

**Formulas:**

- Fg =G(m1+m2)(r)-2
- G = 6.674 * 10-11 Nm2(kg)-2
  - **Gravity near** **Earth’s** **surface [Geophysical Applications]**
- a = g’(rE(r)-1) 2
- mE = 5.98 x 1024 kg
- rE = 6.38 x 106 m
  - **Satellites and “Weightlessness”**

**Newton’s Thought Experiment (Ignore Air Resistance)**

If a projectile launched horizontally is fast enough, the curve of its path will match the surface of any planetary object, namely, Earth.

Gravitational Force provides Centripetal Force (Satellites orbiting Earth)

**Gravitational Field**

Any object with mass is surrounded by a gravitational field in which another object experiences a force due to the interaction between its mass and the gravitational field g at its location.

Unit: N(kg)-1 or m(s)-2

**5-9 Kepler’s 3 Laws** **and Newton’s Synthesis**

**Kepler’s First Law**

The path of each planet about the Sun is an ellipse with the Sun at one focus.

**Kepler’s Second Law**

An imaginary line from the Sun to a planet sweeps out equal areas during equal intervals of time.

**Kepler’s Third Law**

The square of the ratio of the periods is equal to the cube of the ratio of their average distance. (TA /TB)2 = (rA /rB)3
