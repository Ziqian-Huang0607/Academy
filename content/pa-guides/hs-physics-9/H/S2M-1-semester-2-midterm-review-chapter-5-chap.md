## Chapter 5: Circular Motion; Gravitation

## 5.1 Kinematics of Uniform Circular Motion

**Uniform Circular Motion**

-Uniform circular motion: an object that moves in a circle at constant speed is said to experience uniform circular motion

-Velocity vector: always tangent to the path

-Centripetal acceleration (aR): also known as radial acceleration, always points to the center of the circle

-Formula: aR= v2/r= 4π2r/ T2=4π2f2r

-On a rigid body experiencing UCM, two points on the body has the same frequency and period

## 5.2 Dynamics of Uniform Circular Motion

**Centripetal Force**

-Centripetal force (FR or FC): the net force toward the center of the circle during circular motion

-Centripetal force is not a new force, it is the net force of circular motion

-Can be made up of normal force, gravity, tension, etc.

-Formula: Fnet= FR= maR= m v2/r= mω2r= m 4π2r/ T2

**Centrifugal Force**

-Centrifugal force: not an actual force

-the feeling of being pushed away from the center is due to inertia

## 5.3 Highway Curves, Banked and Unbanked

**Unbanked Curve**

-Formula: Fnet= Ffs= FR

-The centripetal force is provided by static friction

-There is never a centrifugal force

-If the friction force is not sufficient, the car will tend to move more nearly in a straight line according to Newton’s 1st law (inertia)

-As long as the tires do not slip, the friction is static

-If the tires start to slip, the friction is kinetic

Consequences:

- The Ffk< Ffs, so the car can’t turn as sharply
- The Ffs points toward the circle center, but the Ffk opposes the direction of motion, making it difficult to regain control of the car and continue around the curve

-Formula for unbanked curve: vmax= √(μsgr)

-Maximum speed is independent of mass

**Banked Curve**

-Banked turns: using a slope/ hill/ angle to help us turn safely

-Banked curves helps keep cards from skidding

-For every banked curve: there is one speed v where no friction is required

-Formula for designed speed in banked curve: v= √(gr tanθ)

-Designed speed is independent of mass

-If v> designed speed: Ffs appears to increase the Fnetx

If v< designed speed: Ffs appears to decrease th Fnetx

-Function of banked turns:

1. To allow us to not rely on friction for turning on a road

2. To allow planes to turn, as they do not have friction

3. To allow race cars to go at faster speeds

4. To help with the wear and tear of your tires

## 5.6 Newton’s Law of Universal Gravitation

**Newton’s Law of Universal Gravitation**

-Statement: Every particle in the universe attracts every other particle with a force that is proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them. This force acts along the line joining the two particles.

-Formula: ${F}_{G}=G\frac{{m}_{1}{m}_{2}}{{r}^{2}}$

-G: universal gravitational constant, G=6.67 x 10-11 N·m2 / kg2

-In 1798, Englishman Henry Cavendish measured the gravitational force between two objects, found universal gravitational constant

-The device was invented by John Michell

-Conditions to apply to calculation

1. Two particles (objects with no size and no shape) separated distance r away

2. Extended objects: small size compared to the distance r

3. Extended objects: when r and the sizes are comparable, integral calculus is needed

-Newton was able to show that the gravitational force exerted on a particle outside a uniform sphere is the same as if the entire mass of the sphere was concentrated at its center.

## 5.8 Satellites and “Weightlessness”

**Newton’s thought** **experiment**

-Ignore air resistance

-Projectile launched horizontally

-If the horizontal speed v is high enough, curve of its path matches the surface of the Earth. The cannonball would be able to orbit the Earth

**Speed and period for a satellite**

-Satellites are put into orbit at very high speeds, this way, as it falls towards earth, it is also moving horizontally, causing it to not crash into Earth.

-A satellite in an orbit that is always the same height above earth moves in uniform circular motion

-Gravitational force provides the centripetal force

-Formulas: speed- v= √(G mcentral/ r)

Period- T= 2π√(r3/ G mcentral)

-The “weightlessness” experienced by people in a satellite orbit close to earth is the same apparent weightlessness experienced in a free fall elevator. Weight is still present, but the apparent weight, or normal force is 0. Astronauts experience weightlessness as the shuttle and everything in it falls freely towards Earth together.

**Gravitational field**

-Any object with mass is surrounded by a gravitational field in which another object experiences a force due to the interaction between its mass and the gravitational field g at its location.

-Calculations for gravitational field forces is the same as calculations for the gravitational acceleration

-Formula: gravitational field g= GM/ r2

Unit: N/kg or m/s2

**6: Work and Energy**

**6-1 & 6-2Work done by a varying/constant force**

Work: the product of the force component parallel to the displacement and the magnitude of the displacement

-Scalar(no direction), but can be pos/neg

Symbol: W

Unit: Joule (J)

Formula for work:

W= Fdcosθ

In this formula, θ is the angle between the direction of the force and the displacement

-When a force is perpendicular to the displacement, no work is done by that force.

Work done by a varying force:

Found by the graphical method - draw an F-d graph

-The area between the line and the d axis is the work done by that force

Area above d axis: positive

Area below d axis: negative

**6-3: Kinetic Energy and Work-Energy Principle**

Energy: The ability of an object to produce a change in itself or the world around

Translational kinetic energy represents the energy due to movement through space, while

kinetic energy implies both translational kinetic energy and rotational kinetic energy.

Translational Kinetic energy:

-The energy of motion

Symbol: KE

Unit: J(Joule)

Work energy principle:

- When all work done on the system is converted into kinetic energy: W=ΔKE

-The net work done on an object is equal to the object’s kinetic energy

-Only valid if W is the net work done

**6-4: Potential Energy**

Potential energy: The energy associated with forces that depend on the position or

configuration of an object relative to the surroundings

Please note that PA materials are student-created and for reference only.

1. Gravitational potential energy

- The stored energy in a system resulting from the gravitational force on the object

Symbol: GPE

Formula: GPE=mgh

m: mass

g: gravitational acceleration

h: height above reference level

2. Elastic potential energy

-The stored energy in an object as a result of its change in shape

-Can be stored in springs, rubber bands, etc...

Hooke’s Law:

The spring force is directly proportional to the displacement if the spring and opposite to the

direction of the displacement of the spring

Formula: FS=-kx

k: spring constant, unit: N/m

x: displacement (compressed or stretched distance)

Spring force is a restoring force: the force is in the direction opposite to the displacement,

restoring the spring to its natural length

Formula for elastic potential energy:

EPE=(k(x)2)/2

-A point with zero PEe (the spring’s natural position), should be chosen as reference point
